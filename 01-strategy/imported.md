# Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** To become the quiet, permanent "emotional immune system" for people who have overcome acute anxiety—empowering them to navigate life's friction with self-knowledge, sustained clarity, and zero digital overwhelm.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Customer Segment: Post-acute mental wellness seekers (people who have resolved their initial crisis and want low-friction emotional maintenance).

Geography: Primary focus on English-speaking mobile markets (US, UK, CA, AU).

Use Cases & Surfaces: Private daily micro-reflections, evening wind-down rituals, and ambient stress prevention on mobile (iOS/Android) and home-screen widgets. Strictly non-social, strictly private.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** Subtractive, trust-first design paired with a personalized "Self-Knowledge Bank." Unlike competitors who push high-velocity engagement loops, streak mechanics, and bloated content libraries, Fable wins by delivering maximum reflection value in under 90 seconds a day—building a deeply personal, searchable memory bank of past resilience that creates high lock-in through self-discovery rather than gamification.
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** World-Class Minimalist UX: Expertise in ultra-low-friction interaction design that makes 60-second reflection feel restorative, not tedious.

Contextual Nudging Engine: Smart algorithmic delivery that triggers reflection prompts based on user-defined rhythms rather than intrusive notification spam.

Longitudinal Synthesis: Machine learning capabilities that organize, theme, and surface past journaling data into meaningful personal growth insights over months and years.
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** Primary Metric (North Star): Healthy Retention Rate (percentage of active users engaging 2–4 times per week after 90+ days without notifications enabled).

Anti-Metrics: App time-spent (lower is better for single sessions) and push-notification tap-through rate (to prevent reliance on noisy re-engagement).

Quarterly Rituals: "Simplicity Audits" to strip out redundant features before adding new ones, paired with quarterly retention cohort reviews focused specifically on post-acute user lifecycles.

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** Gamified Streak Tracking and Engagement Notifications

We explicitly refuse to build streak counters, variable reward loops, or aggressive push notifications to drive daily active usage.

While gamification successfully boosts short-term retention and daily active metrics for competitors, it fundamentally undermines the trust of post-acute users. For someone maintaining mental wellness, missing a "daily streak" creates anxiety, guilt, and a sense of failure—transforming a sanctuary into a taskmaster. Choosing to abandon these high-converting mechanics protects our core differentiator: a low-friction, subtractive experience built on genuine trust and self-knowledge rather than artificial addiction loops.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Transform Fable into the category-defining post-acute emotional maintenance routine by driving deep, trust-based long-term retention without relying on engagement hacks.
- **KR1:** Core Habit: Increase the 90-day retention rate of post-acute users engaging 2–4 times per week from 18% to 35%.
- **KR2:** Self-Knowledge Value: Increase the percentage of active users who view or search their past reflections (60+ days old) at least once per month from 12% to 40%.
- **KR3:** Subtractive Trust: Reduce average daily session time from 4.5 minutes to under 90 seconds while increasing post-session user sentiment scores from 72% to 90% positive.

## AI pressure-test
- **Which challenge from the AI is most valid, and why?:** The Board Question regarding LTV and defensibility. Decreasing session time to under 90 seconds while eliminating notification hooks creates a severe risk of product "invisibility." If users do not perceive distinct, compounding value in those 90 seconds, Fable becomes indistinguishable from a standard built-in journaling app.
- **What would you change based on the pushback, and what would you defend?:** What to Change:

Refine the Hard No: Shift from "No push notifications" to "No guilt-based or streak-based re-engagement." We will allow contextual, value-adding ambient nudges (e.g., a Sunday reflection summary) to maintain top-of-mind awareness without inducing anxiety.

Upgrade KR3: Replace "Reduce session time" with a value-density metric: "Increase 30-day reflection completion rate during self-initiated sessions from 45% to 80% while keeping average session duration under 2 minutes."

What to Defend:

The Subtractive Product Philosophy: Defend rejecting gamification, public feeds, and streak counters. Gamification fundamentally breaks trust with post-acute users.

The "Self-Knowledge Bank" Differentiator: The long-term lock-in is not session length—it is the structured, searchable memory bank of past resilience that a generic notes app cannot synthesize or surface intelligently over time.
