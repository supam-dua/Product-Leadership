# Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:
- To become the quiet, permanent "emotional immune system" for people who have overcome acute anxiety—empowering them to navigate life's friction with self-knowledge, sustained clarity, and zero digital overwhelm.

Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:

- Customer Segment: Post-acute mental wellness seekers (people who have resolved their initial crisis and want low-friction emotional maintenance).

- Geography: Primary focus on English-speaking mobile markets (US, UK, CA, AU).

- Use Cases & Surfaces: Private daily micro-reflections, evening wind-down rituals, and ambient stress prevention on mobile (iOS/Android) and home-screen widgets. Strictly non-social, strictly private.
  
How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?	
- Subtractive, trust-first design paired with a personalized "Self-Knowledge Bank." Unlike competitors who push high-velocity engagement loops, streak mechanics, and bloated content libraries, Fable wins by delivering maximum reflection value in under 90 seconds a day—building a deeply personal, searchable memory bank of past resilience that creates high lock-in through self-discovery rather than gamification.

Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?	
- World-Class Minimalist UX: Expertise in ultra-low-friction interaction design that makes 60-second reflection feel restorative, not tedious.
- Contextual Nudging Engine: Smart algorithmic delivery that triggers reflection prompts based on user-defined rhythms rather than intrusive notification spam.

- Longitudinal Synthesis: Machine learning capabilities that organize, theme, and surface past journaling data into meaningful personal growth insights over months and years.

Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.	
- Primary Metric (North Star): Healthy Retention Rate (percentage of active users engaging 2–4 times per week after 90+ days without notifications enabled).

- Anti-Metrics: App time-spent (lower is better for single sessions) and push-notification tap-through rate (to prevent reliance on noisy re-engagement).

- Quarterly Rituals: "Simplicity Audits" to strip out redundant features before adding new ones, paired with quarterly retention cohort reviews focused specifically on post-acute user lifecycles.
