# Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** To become the quiet, permanent "emotional immune system" for people who have overcome acute anxiety—empowering them to navigate life's friction with self-knowledge, sustained clarity, and zero digital overwhelm.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Customer Segment: Post-acute mental wellness seekers (people who have resolved their initial crisis and want low-friction emotional maintenance).
